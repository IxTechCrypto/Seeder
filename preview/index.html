Seeder Flasher — UI Design Concepts

Three curated visual architectures for the standalone LilyGO USB flashing executable.

1. Cyberpunk 2077 Inspired
Netrunner / Tactical HUD

High-contrast tactical netrunner HUD with pitch black textured carbon, aggressive canary yellow hazard stripes, and monospace telemetry readouts.

  • Colors: Canary Yellow (#fcee0a), Pitch Black, Neon Red accents
  • Controls: 45-degree chamfered buttons with hazard diagonal stripes
  • Panels: Asymmetrical card containers, barcode serial IDs, LED blocks
  • Telemetry: Integrated right-hand terminal with colored log tags
2. TRON: Legacy Inspired
The Digital Grid / Identity Disc

Pure digital grid aesthetic featuring sleek obsidian glass, razor-thin luminescent ice blue circuits, and an animated concentric identity disc progress meter.

  • Colors: Obsidian Black (#020408), Ice Blue / Cyan (#00f3ff), Glowing White
  • Progress: Concentric glowing Identity Disc circular progress meter
  • Schematic: Vector wireframe schematic of LilyGO board and USB pinout
  • Feel: Surgical precision, calm luminescence, zero visual clutter
3. Apple Liquid Glass Minimalist
visionOS / macOS Glassmorphism

Ultra-refined liquid glass frosted acrylic with specular edge highlights, soft diffused shadows, and pristine San Francisco typography.

  • Colors: Midnight Space Gray (#0d0f14), Frosted Acrylic Glass, Pure White
  • Device Card: Centered frosted glass card with 3D LilyGO render & status pill
  • Controls: Floating pill buttons with subtle glass reflection
  • Feel: Hyper-clean, premium consumer electronics simplicity
