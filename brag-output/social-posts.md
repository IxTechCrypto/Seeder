## 📘 Facebook

**Post Copy:**

When creating a Bitcoin cold storage seed phrase, the most critical step is also the most dangerous: trusting the device’s internal random number generator (RNG). If the silicon has subtle flaws, bugs, or backdoors, your funds are compromised from day one.

That’s why **SEEDER** was created.

SEEDER is a DIY, air-gapped sovereign Bitcoin seed generator built on LilyGO ESP32 hardware that completely removes the internal random number generator from the equation. Instead, you supply 100% of the randomness yourself through physical dice rolls or coin flips.

The device simply computes the deterministic BIP-39 math in front of you. Even better, it displays the exact raw hexadecimal entropy on screen so you can cross-check it on any offline computer using standard terminal commands (`sha256sum`). If the math doesn't match, you discard the device.

With zero flash persistence, active memory zeroization on exit, and uninitialized radios (no Wi-Fi, no Bluetooth), SEEDER is the purest "don't trust, verify" device for Bitcoin self-custody.

Read the hardware guide, 3D print your case, and flash the firmware:
